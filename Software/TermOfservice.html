Terms of Service

Last updated: 12 October 2026

What you can do

  • Create and join boards with anyone who has the code
  • Pin, edit, and delete your own notes
  • Share your board code however you want
  • Use Calend for free

What you can't do

  • Spam, harass, or abuse other people on a board
  • Post illegal content, or other people's private information
  • Try to break into Calend or manipulate the database
  • Claim you built Calend if you didn't

Boards are public to those with the code

Anyone with the code can see, pin, edit, and delete notes on that board, including notes written by other people. Calend doesn't verify who wrote a note, and a board code can't be changed or revoked. Only share a code with people you trust, and don't put sensitive information in a note.

Your content and reporting problems

You're responsible for what you pin, and by pinning a note you confirm you're allowed to share it. I may remove notes or delete boards that break these terms, without notice. If you see something illegal or abusive, email me the board code and what the note says, and I'll review it and remove it promptly.

Who can use Calend

You must be at least 13 years old to use Calend.

No warranty

Calend is provided as-is. Notes might disappear due to bugs, database issues, or my mistakes. To the extent allowed by law, I'm not liable for data loss. Back up important stuff elsewhere.

I can change or shut down Calend

If I stop maintaining it or shut it down, your data goes with it. The code is open source, so you can run your own copy if you want.

No ads, no analytics

Calend has no ads and no analytics, and it doesn't sell your data. The Privacy Policy lists exactly what is stored.

Questions or problems?

Open an issue on GitHub or email me at itsphatto@icloud.com.
